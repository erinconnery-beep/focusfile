For coaches and therapists

A private tool from one session to the next.

01 · In session

Identify and explore the client’s goals, habits, and avoidance patterns.

02 · Build together

At the end of a session, turn the client’s clarity and readiness to act into a Focus File. Their own words become the goal, boundaries, and check-ins they carry between sessions.

03 · Next session

Review the Focus File log to see what was finished, what helped, and what pulled them off across the week, instead of relying on a vague retelling.

  • ✓Task initiation
  • ✓Interrupting avoidance patterns
  • ✓Between-session follow-through
