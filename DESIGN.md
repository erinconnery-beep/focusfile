# Evidence Ledger design system

The selected design is intentionally compact, clinical, and tool-like.

- Palette: charcoal and white, with the existing logo green as the only non-semantic accent. Red is reserved for warnings and errors.
- Typography: system sans-serif for high legibility and dependable offline rendering; monospaced labels identify status and evidence fields.
- Shape: sharp borders and restrained surfaces rather than decorative shadows or oversized rounded cards.
- Density: shorter vertical intervals, compact controls, and visible rules make the workflow easy to scan.
- Scope: the visual system is applied to `index.html`, `focus-file.html`, and all three generated sample files. Wording, content order, and interactions are unchanged.
