Overview

Available for the first time in nearly 40 years. Set along one of the upper Madison River’s most scenic stretches, Madison River Ranch spans 228± acres with a half mile of private river frontage and a spring creek that originates on the property. The terrain is strikingly varied. From the river and spring creek, the property rises over 100 feet to elevated benches with panoramic views of the surrounding mountains. The higher elevations offer scale and unobstructed vistas, while the lower reaches form a rare, protected riparian oasis in a landscape more often defined by wide-open grasslands. The property sits within a documented elk migration corridor and supports abundant wildlife throughout the seasons. While much of the surrounding viewshed is protected by conservation easements or public lands, Madison River Ranch remains unencumbered, offering flexibility for the next owner while standing as a clear conservation candidate given its ecological significance.

The Madison River is regarded as one of the world’s premier fly-fishing destinations, and the wade-only section near Three Dollar Bridge is arguably its most coveted stretch. Positioned in the heart of this legendary water, Madison River Ranch offers a rare chance to own significant river frontage in a valley where properties are often generationally held.
