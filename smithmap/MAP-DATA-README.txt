SMITH RIVER RANCH — MAP DATA

The interactive map includes a record-matched complete-ranch candidate, one
verified cadastral parcel and a prepared slot for water data:

0. data/section16-boundary.geojson
   Included. This is the Montana cadastral parcel containing the Ranch House
   and Caretaker's House: S16, T13 N, R04 E, ALL; 637.871 GIS-mapped acres.
   It does not include the Guest House by itself. Source: Montana State Library
   / Montana Department of Revenue Cadastral Framework, tax year 2026.

1. data/ranch-boundary.geojson
   Included. This is a record-matched cadastral candidate assembled from 43
   parcels: all 32 nearby KICKING BULL LLC parcels plus 11 adjacent/associated
   GALT RANCH LP parcels. The geometry totals 21,472.54 GIS-mapped acres versus
   the stated 21,497± acre offering; its tax-record acreage totals 21,515.795.
   It contains all three confirmed house locations and is rendered as the
   translucent gold complete-ranch outline. It is not yet seller/title verified.

2. data/waterways.geojson
   Included. Public reference hydrography from the Montana State Library / USGS
   December 2023 high-resolution NHD snapshot, clipped to the record-matched
   ranch candidate. To keep the marketing map legible, linework is limited to
   named ranch waters matched in both the public data and ranch marketing
   record: Smith River, Eagle Creek and Spring Creek. Public-data ponds and
   spring/seep points inside the candidate are also included. Sheep Creek,
   Cottonwood Creek and Horse Creek remain unmapped until source geometry can
   be confidently matched.

3. data/parcels.geojson
   Included. The 43 individual cadastral features represented by the dissolved
   ranch candidate. This layer is optional and hidden by default.

4. data/public-lands.geojson
   Included. Montana State Trust Land, BLM, Montana FWP and US Forest Service
   reference polygons from the earlier parcel research. This context layer is
   optional and hidden by default. Public ownership does not imply public
   access through the ranch.
