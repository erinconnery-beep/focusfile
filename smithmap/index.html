Location & Access

For all its wild beauty and privacy, the ranch is remarkably easy to reach

Smith River Ranch lies north of White Sulphur Springs, with Bozeman serving as both the primary gateway to the ranch and a destination in its own right.

Elk & Wildlife

A landscape large enough to hold the rhythms of the wild

At times, more than 500 elk range across the ranch’s river bottom, timber and high country.

Abundant springs and tributary streams flow through the ranch and into the Smith River, sustaining a private oasis where wildlife thrives.

Equestrian & Recreation
