Elk, Hunting & Wildlife

A landscape large enough to hold the rhythms of the wild.

A resident herd exceeding 500 elk uses the ranch's river bottom, timber and high country. Mule deer, whitetail and antelope extend the big-game experience, while upland birds move through grass, timber and creek bottoms.
