For the Serious Buyer

Five questions, answered.

01
How big — and how private?
21,497± contiguous deeded acres in one unbroken block. No state, BLM, or leased land anywhere in it. A complete river-valley-to-mountain landscape you can cross all day without leaving.
02
What about the water?
Six miles of the Smith — the only permit-protected river in Montana — both banks for five. Plus 6.75 mi of Sheep Creek and Eagle, Spring, Cottonwood & Horse Creeks and ponds. 93 appurtenant water rights convey.
03
Is it turnkey?
Yes. 100,000± sq ft under roof — a 12,124 sq ft lodge, main house, six guest cabins, plus separately-sited staff and agricultural operations — conveyed substantially furnished and equipped, including the cattle herd.
04
How do I get there?
25± minutes by helicopter from Bozeman Yellowstone International. A jet-capable 6,100-ft airstrip at White Sulphur Springs, 30 minutes out. Just under two hours by road.
05
What conveys?
Water and mineral rights convey at closing. A 227-acre conservation easement (Montana Land Reliance); the remaining 21,270± acres unencumbered. Price upon request.
